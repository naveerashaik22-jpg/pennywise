Money management, made simpler.

PennyWise is a personal finance management application designed to make tracking money simple, organized and accessible. Take charge of your finances, one transaction at a time.

About PennyWise

PennyWise helps individuals monitor their income and expenses through an easy-to-use dashboard. Users can record transactions, organize spending into categories and view their overall financial balance.

Our Mission

To encourage smarter financial habits by providing a simple platform for tracking everyday money activities and understanding personal spending patterns.

What You Can Do

🔐 Personal Accounts

Create an account and access your own dashboard.

📊 Financial Overview

Monitor total income, expenses and available balance.

📝 Transaction Management

Add, view and delete your transactions.

🔍 Smart Search

Search and filter transactions by category.

💾 Local Storage

Keep your information saved in your browser.

📱 Responsive Interface

Use PennyWise across desktop and mobile screens.

Technology Stack

HTML5: Application structure and forms.
CSS3: Modern styling and responsive design.
JavaScript: Application logic and calculations.
Local Storage: Browser-based data persistence.
